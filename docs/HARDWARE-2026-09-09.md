## Generator RF tests and settling fix

The initial connection failed USB enumeration (`error -71`, path `1-2`). After
reconnection, the generator appeared as `1209:c550`, CH55xduino, serial `CH55x`,
`/dev/ttyACM2`. Readback identified OCXO HW 1.3 / SW Jul 10 2025, profile 1,
10 MHz reference, multiplier 270. Its original outputs were channel 1
14.075 MHz, channel 2 25 MHz and channel 3 24 MHz.

The receiver stayed at 14.074 MHz except for the explicit +49 Hz fine-tuning
check. The documented HF connection uses generator channel 2 through 40 dB
attenuation. Channel 1 was parked at 100 MHz during the tests to avoid its
original 14.075 MHz setting contaminating the same tone through another input.
Both changed outputs were restored with verified readback after each test run;
channel 3, profile, multiplier and reference were preserved.

The first RF run confirmed both sidebands, source-away/back behavior and the
49 Hz fine-tuning step. It also caught 22 clipped samples in the first 16 ms of
one immediate post-retune recording. That capture remains in `rf/`. Firmware
now processes but discards the first 2,400 complex samples (100 ms) after every
configuration, allowing chip/DSP filters to settle before filling the audio
queue. After reflash, the complete ten-capture RF sequence was repeated with
no clipping, faults, underruns, overruns or USB stalls in any sampled status.

Final run, using 1 Hz FFT bins at the programmed 12 kHz rate:

| Check | USB | LSB |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Generator wanted frequency | 14.075 MHz | 14.073 MHz |
| Measured audio beat | 950 Hz | 1050 Hz |
| Nominal beat-frequency error | −50 Hz | +50 Hz |
| Source-away drop in wanted-tone search band | 98.0 dB | 107.2 dB |
| Source-return amplitude difference | 0.28 dB | 0.10 dB |
| Suppression when the same RF signal is received in the wrong mode | 76.6 dB | 79.7 dB |

Moving the USB dial from 14.074000 to 14.074049 MHz shifted the measured tone
from 950 to 901 Hz; returning the dial restored 950 Hz. This verifies the 49 Hz
digital tuning step independently of the roughly 50 Hz combined absolute
frequency error. The error corresponds to about 3.6 ppm at this frequency;
the measurement does not isolate receiver-crystal error from source accuracy.

Acceptance thresholds were ±100 Hz nominal beat error, ≥20 dB spectral tone
prominence, ≥15 dB wrong-mode suppression, ≥10 dB source-away drop, ≤3 dB
return difference, no clipped PCM and no nonzero sampled counters in the final
run. The fine-tuning check allowed ±2 Hz error in the 49 Hz shift. All pass.
These are strong-tone, short measurements on the documented HF input, not
calibrated sensitivity, a full RF-range test, independent sample-clock
measurement, or 30-minute sustained qualification. Spectral dB values are
relative digital measurements, not dBm or calibrated RF SNR. Reconfiguration
also changes measured tone level somewhat; it is not an amplitude calibration.

Final receiver state: **14.074000 MHz USB, armed**. Status immediately after
closing SWD tracing was all-zero; after restoring desktop audio it became
`ZZST1,00,0000000000,0000000000,0000000001,0000000000;` (one FIFO overrun).
This reproduces a lifecycle-associated overrun outside the controlled RF
recordings. Its cause remains unisolated; the firmware clears the queue and
recovers. The native RF captures and 60-second capture had no such losses.
Generator outputs are restored to **14.075 / 25 / 24 MHz**. All serial/SWD
handles opened by the test scripts are closed; the receiver's PipeWire Mono
Input profile is restored. No generator reset or firmware change was issued.
